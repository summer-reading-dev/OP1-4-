Owner's Manual

Model SR‑4  ·  Four Track Cassette Recorder

Thank you for selecting the SUMMER READING SR-4. To obtain the finest performance from this instrument, please read these instructions completely before operation, and retain them for future reference.

1

Before You Begin

The SR-4 operates entirely within your web browser. Nothing is uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any server — your audio never leaves the machine in front of you. Consequently, no account, installation, or network connection is required for normal operation.

Note

The unit is supplied without audio. Load your own .wav files into the four tracks, or record directly into them from a microphone — see section 3.

2

Loading Tape

Audio may be loaded into the transport by any of three methods:

  1. LOAD WAV FILES — select one or several files at once.
  2. Drag and drop onto the deck to fill the next available track.
  3. Drag onto a single track to replace only that track's contents.

Files named in the pattern track_1.wav through track_4.wav are routed automatically to their corresponding track. Files bearing no such number are placed into the next empty track in sequence.

Caution

This model accepts uncompressed .wav files only. Compressed formats such as MP3 or M4A will be refused on loading.

3

Recording

Any track may be recorded directly from a microphone or instrument input. Press the ● button on a track to arm it; the track is outlined in red and its meter shows the incoming signal, allowing levels to be set before a take.

Press ● RECORD to begin. The remaining tracks play back as you record, so parts may be overdubbed one upon another in the usual manner. Press STOP, or allow playback to reach the end of the selection, to finish. The take is placed on the armed track and behaves thereafter exactly as a loaded file.

Note

Only one track may be armed at a time, the instrument having a single input. Arming a second track releases the first.

Count-In

Engaging COUNT-IN 4 in the tempo row sounds four bars of clicks before each take, so that you may come in on the beat. The first beat of every bar is accented. The count follows your tempo and time signature — four bars of three at 120 will count twelve — and the bar and beat are displayed as it proceeds.

Recording begins only when the count has finished, so no part of it appears in the take. STOP during the count abandons it without recording.

Note

A tempo must be set for the count-in to operate. Enter a BPM or use TAP.

Recording begins at the playback position, so a passage may be dropped into the middle of an arrangement rather than always begun from the head of the tape. The unit measures the output delay of your audio hardware and advances the take accordingly, so that an overdub sits in time with what you were listening to.

Caution

Recording onto a track that already carries audio will replace it. You will be asked to confirm. The input is metered but never fed to the loudspeakers, as this would return to the microphone as feedback — use headphones when overdubbing, or the microphone will capture the playback along with your performance.

4

Track Controls

Each of the four tracks is provided with an identical control complement:

Title fieldNames the track. Appears on the Reels export. Maximum 20 characters.
VOLTrack level, 0 to 1.30. Levels above 1.00 provide gain for quiet material.
PANStereo placement, full left to full right.
●Arms the track for recording. See section 3.
MMute. Silences this track without disturbing its level setting.
SSolo. When any track is soloed, all non‑soloed tracks are silenced.
⬇Downloads this track alone as a .wav file.
5

The Waveform Displays

Every track carries its own waveform display, as does the master mix. These are not merely indicators — they are the principal editing surface of the instrument.

Click or dragMoves playback to that point, during playback or at rest.
Ctrl / ⌘ + scrollZooms in for finer editing. Pinch gestures are also recognised.
ScrollOnce zoomed, pans along the recording.
FITReturns the display to the full length of the track.

The strip beneath the master display is a minimap — drag it to travel quickly through a long mix.

6

Master Mix — Trim & Fades

The trim selection determines what is heard on playback and what is written on mixdown. Everything outside it is discarded.

Amber circlesDrag to set the start and end of the selection.
White squaresAt mid‑height. Drag inward from either end to set a fade‑in or fade‑out across the whole mix.
RESET TRIMRestores the selection to the full length of the longest track.
7

Tempo & Bar Grid

Setting a tempo lays a bar grid across the master display and every track, for editing reference only. It does not alter the audio in any way.

BPMEnter a tempo directly. Accepted range 20 to 300.
TAPTap in time with the music to derive the tempo. Averaged over your last eight taps; pausing more than about two seconds begins a fresh count.
/ BARBeats per bar, for material not in four.
GRIDShows or hides the grid without discarding the tempo.
COUNT-IN 4Sounds four bars of clicks before each take. See section 3.
8

Master EQ & Output

Three shelving and peaking bands — LOW, MID, HIGH — act upon the combined output. FLAT returns all three to 0 dB. The equalisation is applied both to what you hear and to the finished mixdown.

The OUTPUT display is a live spectrum analyser, presented in the manner of a graphic‑equaliser display of the period. It is an indicator only and has no effect upon the signal.

9

Transport

◀◀ REWReturns to the start of the trim selection.
▶ PLAYPlays the trim selection.
■ STOPHalts playback.
⟳ LOOPRepeats the trim selection indefinitely.
● RECORDRecords the armed track while the others play back. Press again, or STOP, to finish.
MASTEROverall output level, below the transport.
10

Mixdown

MIXDOWN → WAV renders the trim selection to a single stereo .wav file at 44.1 kHz, 16‑bit. Track levels, panning, mutes and solos, the master level, the master EQ, and any fades are all applied. Rendering is performed offline and is therefore faster than real time.

Note

Should the NOTES panel contain any text, a matching .txt file is downloaded alongside the mix — useful for lyrics, credits, or session notes.

11

Session Storage

The SR-4 preserves your work by two independent means.

Automatic

Titles, notes, reading list, every fader, pan, mute, solo, the master level, EQ, tempo, time signature, grid and loop state are retained in this browser and restored when you return. The audio itself is not retained — recordings are far too large — so a restored session returns as a fully configured deck awaiting its tapes.

Session Files

SAVE SESSION writes everything — audio included, together with trim and fade positions — to a single .4trk file. This file may be archived, copied to another machine, or given to a collaborator. OPEN SESSION restores it entire.

Caution

Session files carry uncompressed audio and are correspondingly large. A full four‑track arrangement of several minutes may exceed 100 MB. Allow time for saving and opening.

12

Reels Export

The SR-4 will render a vertical video of your mix, suitable for social distribution. A still photograph may be placed behind it, or a video, which plays muted beneath the mix.

Format720 × 1280, vertical (9:16).
DurationYour trim selection, to a maximum of 30 seconds.
Footage over 30sCuts to a random five‑second passage from elsewhere in the footage every five seconds, rather than merely playing the opening.
Reading listUp to four titles, one per line, lettered onto the artwork.
No mediaGenerated artwork is substituted.
13

Keyboard Operation

1 2 3 4Selects a track for keyboard control.
↑ ↓Raises or lowers the selected track's level.
← →Pans the selected track.
EscDeselects.

Keyboard control is suspended while typing in any text field.

14

In Case of Difficulty

No sound on PLAYConfirm audio is loaded, the track is not muted, and that no other track is soloed.
A file will not loadThe unit accepts uncompressed .wav only. Convert compressed material first.
RECORD refusedNo track is armed. Press the ● button on the track you wish to record.
Count-in will not runNo tempo is set. Enter a BPM or use TAP.
Microphone deniedPermission was refused. Allow microphone access for this page in your browser's site settings, then arm the track again.
Take sounds hollow, or doubledThe loudspeakers are being recorded along with the performance. Use headphones.
Mixdown refusedThe trim selection is empty. Widen it and repeat.
Settings not retainedPrivate browsing, or a browser set to block site data, prevents storage. Use a session file instead.
Reel export refusedYour browser does not provide the necessary video recorder. A current Chrome, Edge or Firefox is advised.
